3. Putting it on your site

  1. Open the page on your club website where the sponsor should appear.
  2. Add a custom HTML, embed or code block.
  3. Paste the code above and save. Reload the page to check.

What it shows: only the sponsor's name, logo, your headline and message, and the button, taken from campaigns you have published for the website. Never rides, riders, phone numbers or anything private. Changes you make under Sponsors reach the widget within about 5 minutes.

If your website builder won't take it: some free or locked-down website builders do not allow custom HTML or iframes, and some block embedded content from other sites. This widget is an iframe, so it won't work there. In that case you can add the sponsor's logo to your site by hand, or share the ride sheet link, which always carries the sponsor panel. We can't promise it works on every builder.

The widget is a plain page: no scripts, no cookies, and a strict content policy, so it can't run anything on your site. It needs about 300 pixels of height on a phone; make the box taller if your sponsor's text is long.

The widget uses no scripts and no cookies. It does count that it was requested (not who by), as described in Reports.
