Bring a route you've made on Strava into Ride Leader Kit so you can add cafés, regroups and stops and export it to your bike computer. Here's exactly what that involves.

What we read

  • The list of your routes (name, distance, climbing and a small outline), including private routes if you allow it on Strava.
  • The route file for any route you choose to import.
  • Your Strava athlete number, so we can tell which routes are yours.

How

You'll approve access on Strava's own page. It is read-only: we can't create, change or delete anything on Strava.

How long, and who sees it

We keep it for at most 7 days, then it's deleted automatically. Only you can see it here. If you want a route to stay, you can share your own copy with your club (you'll be asked to confirm it's yours).

Changing your mind

  • Withdraw consent: press Disconnect Strava on your account page, or remove the app in your Strava settings. Either way your Strava data is deleted and we email you to confirm.
  • Ask for deletion: use Delete my account on the same page, or contact the club.
