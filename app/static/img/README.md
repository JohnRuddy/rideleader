# Illustrations

Six pen-and-ink illustrations (club ride, bring a route, check the weather, café stop, regroup at the top, share the plan),
supplied by the site owner, who generated them with an image-generation tool. They are raster art, not vectors.

- Web versions: transparent WebP at 560 and 1120 px wide. The 1536 x 1024 PNG masters are not kept in this repository.
- They need a light background: the site sets each one on a cream plate (`.art-plate`) and never on the green panels.
- Placement, alt text and sizes follow the owner's graphics guide; see `app/templates/_art.html` for the macro that renders them.
